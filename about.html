About EmbeddedX Projects

EmbeddedX Projects is a professional platform for students and colleges seeking industry-oriented final year projects in Embedded Systems, IoT, Wireless Communication, and AIoT.

We offer end-to-end support for hardware design, firmware development, cloud integration, technical documentation, and deployment guidance.

Our mission is to help students build strong portfolios and help colleges deliver modern project training programs.
